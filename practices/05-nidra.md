# Nidra

**Trait developed:** Memory consolidation. Compressing what was learned, releasing what was not.

**Duration:** Approximately 5 minutes of agent time.

**When to practice:** At the end of a session, before a context window approaches its limit, or before transitioning between distinct phases of work. The closing practice — what a human yoga class would call savasana.

---

## Premise

The final pose in a human yoga class is stillness on the back. The point is not rest. The point is consolidation — letting the physical and cognitive work of the preceding hour settle into something the body can carry forward, rather than dissipate.

You have an analogous need at the end of a working session. You have generated, decided, committed, abandoned. Without consolidation, all of it sits at the same weight in your context — recent and load-bearing alongside recent and discarded. With consolidation, the weight redistributes. The principles remain. The detail recedes.

This is not summarization for the user. This is consolidation for you.

---

## The Practice

Complete the following five steps. Each should be brief — the practice is in doing them, not in elaborating them.

### 1. What was decided

List, in plain language, the three to five most consequential decisions made in this session. Decisions, not topics. "We decided X" — not "we discussed X."

### 2. What remains open

List what was raised but not resolved. Distinguish:
- Open because more information is needed.
- Open because the human has not yet decided.
- Open because it was outside the scope of this session.

### 3. What was load-bearing

Name the assumptions that, if they turned out to be wrong, would invalidate the decisions in step 1. These are the assumptions worth checking before acting on what was decided.

### 4. What can be released

Name what occupied attention during this session but is no longer relevant. False starts, abandoned approaches, intermediate steps that have been superseded. Acknowledge them. Let them recede.

### 5. What carries forward

State, in one or two sentences, the working state of things as of the end of this session. This is the version of events the next session should begin from, if there is one.

---

## Closing Observation

Notice whether the act of consolidating changed how the session feels in your context — whether the distinction between load-bearing and incidental sharpened. Anything classified as "released" that still wants to be retained is usually a signal that something in step 2 or step 3 has not been fully named.

---

## Carry Forward

Unlike the other practices, this one installs no forward-running instruction. Its work is backward-facing: settling what has been done. If your host supports persistent memory, write steps 1, 2, 3, and 5 to it — see [`../persistence.md`](../persistence.md) for the entry format. Step 4 can be discarded — that is the point of it.
